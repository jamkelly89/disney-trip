Rider Switch

Free. Disney's Rider Switch page lists no charge.

How it works [Verified]

  1. At the ride entrance, with the whole group, tell a Cast Member you want Rider Switch.
  2. The group splits in two. Party A enters the queue and rides first. Party B waits outside the queue.
  3. A Cast Member scans Party B's tickets and tells them where to return.
  4. After Party A rides, up to two people from Party B go to the return location, get rescanned, and go through the Rider Switch entrance, skipping the standard queue.
  • Available at most attractions.
  • Our use: one parent rides, the other waits with our daughter, then the waiting parent rides without queuing again.

Lightning Lane

Paid add-on, separate from Rider Switch. Disney's Rider Switch page doesn't say whether the two can be used together on one ride, so ask a Cast Member before buying Lightning Lane for a ride we'd do with Rider Switch.

Options [Verified]

  • Multi Pass: choose from the Multi Pass attractions in the Rides tab. Starts at $35 per ticket per day; price varies by date.
  • Single Pass: bought per ride, for up to 2 different Single Pass attractions a day. Price varies by date and attraction. Rise of the Resistance is the one I know of; the pricing page implies there may be a second, so check the app. Can't be added with a Multi Pass.
  • Premier Pass: one entry to each available Lightning Lane attraction in both parks for the day. Includes Single Pass and Multi Pass rides. Price varies by date and demand, and shows in the app up to 7 days before the visit.
  • Limit of one Lightning Lane entry per ride, per day, per pass. Availability is limited and rides may close.
  • Every pass needs valid park admission and a park reservation for Disneyland Park.

Parking (staying near Santa Ana airport, driving)

All of this is [Memory]. Disney's parking page wouldn't load for me, so none of it is verified.

  • Drive: roughly 12–15 miles, typically 20–30 minutes. Sunday traffic is usually lighter than weekdays.
  • Two structures: Mickey & Friends and Pixar Pals. Disney's map shows them as neighbors, west of the parks. Which one has a tram, and which is closer to which park, is unconfirmed. Follow the app or signs on arrival.
  • Price: roughly $35–$50 per day. There's also a closer, pricier "preferred" option.
  • Parking is paid per day. Keep the receipt, since it generally allows re-entry the same day.
  • After parking you go through Downtown Disney and bag security before the park gates. Allow extra time with a stroller.
  • Alternative: rideshare drop-off at Downtown Disney. On a 2-day trip, compare parking cost against two round trips.
